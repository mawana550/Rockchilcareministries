Choose how you want to send your donation:

Send through Wave

  1. Open the Wave app or visit the Wave website.
  2. Choose Uganda as the destination and mobile wallet as the delivery method.
  3. Enter the recipient below and send your amount.
  4. Email your receipt to mulwanaian6@gmail.com or send it to our Facebook page.
